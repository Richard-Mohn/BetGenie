# BetGenie — Business Model

*Last Updated: March 2, 2026*

---

## Revenue Streams

### 1. Subscription Tiers (Primary Revenue)

| Tier | Price | Features |
|------|-------|----------|
| **Free** | $0 | 3 player lookups/day, basic Impact Scores (no factor breakdown), 1 game analysis/day, banner ads |
| **Pro** | $29.99/mo ($249/yr) | Unlimited lookups, full Impact Score breakdown, all game analysis, props recommendations, email alerts, bet tracker, ad-free |
| **Elite** | $79.99/mo ($699/yr) | Everything in Pro + Parlay Builder, SMS alerts, advanced filters, priority data refresh, historical backtesting, bankroll management, dedicated support |
| **API** | $199/mo+ | REST API access, webhooks, custom integrations, bulk data exports, usage-based pricing above threshold |

### Target Subscriber Mix (Year 1)
| Tier | Users | MRR |
|------|-------|-----|
| Free | 50,000 | $0 (ad-supported) |
| Pro | 5,000 | $149,950 |
| Elite | 1,000 | $79,990 |
| API | 50 | $9,950 |
| **Total** | **56,050** | **$239,890** |

### Projected Annual Revenue (Year 1): ~$2.9M

---

### 2. Advertising (Secondary Revenue)

- Display ads on free tier: $5-15 CPM
- Sponsored analysis sections (clearly labeled)
- Sportsbook affiliate partnerships
- Estimated: $50K-200K/year from free tier traffic

---

### 3. Affiliate Revenue

- Sportsbook sign-up referrals (DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM, etc.)
- Typical CPA: $100-300 per new depositing customer
- Integrated "Place Bet" CTAs that link to partner sportsbooks
- Estimated: $100K-500K/year depending on traffic

---

### 4. Enterprise / B2B (Phase 5)

- White-label Impact Score API for sportsbooks
- Media company licenses (ESPN, Bleacher Report, etc.)
- Podcast/content creator partnerships
- Custom analytics for professional bettors and syndicates
- Estimated: $500K+/year

## Go-To-Market Strategy

### Phase 2 Launch (NBA MVP)
1. **Sports betting subreddits** (r/sportsbook, r/sportsbetting, r/NBAbetting)
2. **Twitter/X** — Live-tweet analysis before games with Impact Scores
3. **YouTube** — "BetGenie vs The Line" series showing AI predictions vs outcomes
4. **Sports betting podcasts** — Guest appearances, sponsor spots
5. **Content marketing** — SEO-optimized articles on player analysis

### Phase 3 (NFL Launch)
1. **NFL is king** — Massive marketing push for NFL Week 1
2. **Parlay-focused ads** — "Turn your 4-leg parlay into a smart 4-leg parlay"
3. **Influencer partnerships** — Sports betting content creators
4. **Affiliate network** — Content sites, tipsters, Discord communities

### Phase 4 (Scale)
1. **Paid acquisition** — Meta, Google, Twitter ads targeting sports bettors
2. **App Store Optimization** — Keywords, screenshots, reviews
3. **Referral program** — "Give $10, Get $10" for monthly subscription
4. **PR** — Media coverage of the "AI that sees what stats don't"
